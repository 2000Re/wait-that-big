#20 · Space: which is bigger?

Pluto's whole surface or Russia: which is bigger?

Make your guess, then check the answer.

Pluto is bigger!

  • Pluto (a dwarf planet): whole surface about 17.7 million km² (about 2,400 km across)
  • Russia: about 17.1 million km² (total area)

Flat (Mercator) maps stretch land near the poles. In this video the shape is moved across the globe at its true size, and areas are calculated on the Earth's surface (WGS84), not on the flat map. Map data: Natural Earth (public domain).
